Privacy Policy

Channel Studio · Last updated: 20 November 2026

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Channel Studio ("Studio", "we") is a tool that helps creators make short videos, publish them to their own Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts, read their performance, and answer viewers who ask for something in the comments. This policy covers two groups of people: creators who connect their accounts to Studio, and viewers who comment on or message a creator's account.

Creators: what we collect and why

  • Your Studio login: username and a salted, hashed password. We never store the password itself.
  • Access tokens for the accounts you connect with Facebook Login (and Google sign-in for YouTube). Used only to act for you inside Studio; never shown in the app or shared.
  • Your account and Page names and IDs, and your Facebook user ID, so we know which accounts are yours and can honour deletion requests.
  • Your content and its performance: videos you make in Studio, captions, scheduled times, and statistics for your own posts (views, likes, comments, saves, reach, watch time).
  • What you type into Studio (channel description, video instructions, comment-reply settings).

We use this only to provide the features you use: making and publishing your videos, showing your statistics and suggestions, and running the comment replies you set up.

Viewers who comment or message a creator

When a creator sets up a comment keyword on one of their reels, Studio reads new comments on that reel. If you comment the keyword, Studio replies under your comment and sends you one private message. If you reply, Studio checks whether you follow that creator and sends what you asked for. For this we process your Instagram username, Instagram-scoped ID, the comment or message text and time, and whether you follow the creator. We never message people who did not comment the keyword or write first.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data, and don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't read your private messages beyond conversations started from a comment keyword or sent to the connected account.
  • We don't post anything without the creator's approval in the app.

Services we use

  • Meta (Instagram Graph API, Facebook Login) and Google (YouTube Data and Analytics APIs) to act for creators.
  • Anthropic (Claude) to write video scripts and suggestions from the creator's own channel description and statistics. Viewers' comments and messages are not sent to it.
  • ElevenLabs to create narration from the video script.
  • Cloudflare to carry traffic to Studio securely.

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube API Services Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

Storage, security and retention

Data is stored on infrastructure we control, reachable only over HTTPS and only after sign-in; each creator can see only their own accounts. Viewer records (comments and messages handled by keyword replies) are deleted automatically after 90 days. Creator data is kept while the account is connected and deleted when the creator disconnects or asks us to.

How to delete your data

Creators: remove Channel Studio from Facebook (Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations → Remove). Facebook tells us automatically and we delete your connected accounts, tokens, videos and statistics, and give you a confirmation code you can check. You can also disconnect an account inside Studio, or contact us.

Viewers: send the word DELETE in a direct message to the account you commented on. Studio erases its records about you right away and confirms by message.

Any other request: contact us below, and we complete it within 30 days.

Children

Studio is not for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly process their data.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw consent by disconnecting your accounts, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Changes

We'll update the date at the top when this policy changes, and tell connected creators about significant changes inside Studio.
