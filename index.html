Privacy Policy

For the Instagram account @gotitinsec and its tool "Channel Studio". Last updated: 20 November 2026.

@gotitinsec is a creator account that posts short science, tech and general-knowledge reels. Channel Studio is the private tool we use to publish our reels and to answer people who ask for something in the comments. This page explains what Instagram data the tool uses, why, and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect

Only when you interact with one of our reels or messages:

  • Comments on our reels: the comment text, time, your Instagram username and Instagram-scoped user ID.
  • Messages you send us in reply to our message: the text and time.
  • Whether you follow @gotitinsec, checked only after you reply to our message.
  • Basic statistics about our own reels (views, likes, comments, saves), which do not identify you.

We do not collect your email, phone number, location, contacts, password, or anything from your private account content.

How we use it

  • If you comment a keyword we asked for on a reel (for example "HONEY"), we reply to your comment and send you one private message about it.
  • If you reply to that message, we check whether you follow @gotitinsec and send the link or information you asked for. If you don't follow yet, we ask you to follow first.
  • We use the record of who we messaged only to avoid messaging you twice for the same reel.

We never send messages to people who did not comment or write to us first. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Where it is stored and for how long

The data is stored on our own computer, not on a public server, and is only accessible to the account owner. Records of comments and messages are deleted automatically after 90 days. Instagram also keeps its own copy of comments and messages under Instagram's Privacy Policy.

Services we use

We use Meta's Instagram Graph API to read comments, reply, send messages and check follows. Reel scripts are written with the help of Anthropic's Claude; your comments and messages are not sent to it.

How to delete your data

To have everything we hold about you deleted, do either of the following:

  1. Send the word DELETE in a direct message to @gotitinsec on Instagram, or
  2. Write to us through the contact below with your Instagram username.

We delete your records within 30 days and confirm by message. You can also delete your own comments on Instagram at any time, and you can stop all messages by blocking or restricting @gotitinsec.

Children

Our tool is not meant for people under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
